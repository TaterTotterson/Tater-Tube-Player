# Demo artwork generation record

The following project-bound assets were created with Codex's built-in image
generation tool for the rights-safe Steam demo library. Each prompt required
entirely original fictional content and prohibited existing characters, real
performers, brands, logos, readable text, and watermarks.

## Cosmic Drift

> Premium cinematic 3D key art for a fictional science-fiction movie: a small
> explorer spacecraft drifting past a luminous orange nebula and a distant
> ringed planet. Landscape 16:9, central-right focal subject, deep black and
> graphite with warm Tater orange rim light and restrained violet and blue.

Final asset: `assets/demo/cosmic-drift.png`

## Cosmic Drift portrait poster

> Recompose the approved Cosmic Drift landscape artwork into a vertical 2:3
> movie poster. Preserve the spacecraft, orange nebula, ringed planet, lighting,
> and cinematic finish; keep the spacecraft in the lower half and the planet
> above. No title, words, logos, border, watermark, people, or recognizable IP.

Final asset: `assets/demo/cosmic-drift-poster.png`
